Brownies

Description

Bake these easy brownies whenever you need to quickly satisfy your sweet tooth.

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • butter
  • Cocoa Powder

Steps

  1. Mix all the ingredients
  2. Preheat the oven to 175 °C
  3. Spread in a prepared pan
  4. Bake for 20 min

You have the best smooth brownies
